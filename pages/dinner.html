Chicken Parmesan

Chicken Parmesan features crispy breaded chicken topped with tomato sauce, mozzarella cheese and Parmesan cheese. It is a delicious family dinner.

How to Make Chicken Parmesan

  1. Season the chicken breasts with salt and pepper.
  2. Coat each piece in flour, dip it into beaten eggs, and cover it with breadcrumbs mixed with grated Parmesan cheese.
  3. Heat oil in a frying pan and cook the chicken until both sides are golden brown.
  4. Transfer the chicken to a baking dish.
  5. Spoon tomato sauce over each piece of chicken.
  6. Top with mozzarella and extra Parmesan cheese.
  7. Bake in a preheated oven until the cheese is melted and the chicken is fully cooked.
  8. Garnish with fresh parsley or basil if desired.
  9. Serve hot with pasta, salad, or garlic bread.

Chicken Noodle Soup

Chicken noodle soup is made with chicken, vegetables and noodles in a warm broth. It is comforting, healthy and easy to enjoy.

How to Make Chicken Noodle Soup

  1. Heat a little oil in a large pot and cook the onions, carrots, and celery until softened.
  2. Add garlic and cook for another minute.
  3. Pour in the chicken stock and bring it to a boil.
  4. Add the chicken and simmer until it is fully cooked.
  5. Remove the chicken, shred it into bite-sized pieces, and return it to the pot.
  6. Add the noodles and cook until they are tender.
  7. Season the soup with salt, pepper, and your preferred herbs.
  8. Stir well and simmer for a few more minutes.
  9. Serve hot with chopped parsley or fresh herbs as a garnish.

Taiwanese Three Cup Chicken

Three Cup Chicken is a Taiwanese dish cooked with soy sauce, sesame oil and rice wine. Fresh basil adds a fragrant and delicious flavor.

How to Make Taiwanese Three Cup Chicken

  1. Cut the chicken into bite-sized pieces and prepare the garlic, ginger, and fresh basil.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and cook the ginger until fragrant.
  3. Add the garlic and cook for another minute.
  4. Add the chicken and stir-fry until lightly browned.
  5. Pour in soy sauce, sesame oil, and rice wine, then stir to coat the chicken evenly.
  6. Allow the sauce to simmer until it thickens and coats the chicken.
  7. Add fresh basil leaves and stir until they begin to wilt.
  8. Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed.
  9. Serve hot with steamed rice.
